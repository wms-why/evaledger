### Added

- **GPT-6.1 Terra** — a new price row, a leaderboard row, a release post (en + zh) and a ledger record, all in one commit: $1 input / $5 output per 1M tokens with a $0.05 cache read, a 1,050,000-token window and 128,000 max output tokens, billed under the API id `gpt-6.1-terra`. The 272,000-token long-context step (2× input, 1.5× output) and the half-price Batch and Flex lanes are encoded on the row, and the knowledge cutoff, the cache-write rates and the Fast-mode multiplier are in the model note.
- **Two scores for Terra, both from OpenAI's own launch material** — Terminal-Bench 4.0 50.3 at max reasoning effort, and Agents' Last Exam 60.0, the max-effort point in OpenAI's cost-versus-score chart. Each note names the setting it was measured at, the chart's own label (Agents' Last Exam v1, while the other rows in that column use the v1.1 task set) and the rival figures OpenAI printed next to it.
- **A new member on the GPT-6 line** — Terra is the 6.1 version of the series, listed first with its own price and score keys, so `/series/gpt-6` now answers "is 6.1 worth switching to?" against astra, sol and luna.
- **A compare pair and a homepage slot** — `gpt-6.1-terra` vs `gpt-6-astra` joins the hand-picked comparisons and leads the homepage's popular list, because it is the question this launch raises: what does the frontier cost when the price drops 10×?
- **A real ship date for Terra: 2026-10-26.** The launch page prints only "2026", but OpenAI's developer changelog carries the dated entry that says "Released GPT-6.1 Terra" and lists it on the Responses and Chat Completions endpoints. With that date the model, the leaderboard row, the price row and the post all carry a ship date, and the post lands on the launch timeline instead of sinking to the end of the list.

### Changed

- **The Agents' Last Exam field best moved, and every other row on the agent board is re-normalized against it.** Terra's 60.0 is the highest figure we hold for that benchmark, so the board's denominator for it is higher than before and the composite scores of the models already on that board are lower. No model got worse; the yardstick moved. This is the one case the methodology allows — a newcomer that becomes the field best on a core benchmark — and the release post says so in plain words rather than letting the numbers shift silently.
- **The price registry's snapshot date moved to 2026-10-27** and its version to 0.10.0; the leaderboard registry to 0.9.0. Every other row is unchanged — OpenAI's own prices for the rest of the line were re-verified against the pricing page on the same day and did not move.

### Unchanged

- **URLs.** Route slugs, the canonical domain and `/api/prices.json` are untouched. GPT-6.1 Terra is a new row in every registry, not an edit to an existing one.

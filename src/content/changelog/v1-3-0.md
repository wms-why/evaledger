### Changed

- **New domain — [evaledger.com](https://evaledger.com)** is now the canonical home of the calculator. Every canonical URL, `hreflang` alternate, `og:url`, the sitemap, `robots.txt` and the RSS feed now point at `evaledger.com`; the previous host still serves the same app for a while, but the canonical link of every page points at the new domain.
- **New name — EvalLedger.** The wordmark, the page titles, the footer, the email templates and the legal pages now say EvalLedger. The product itself is unchanged: the same live tokenizer, the same per-token price table across OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and Grok, the same OpenRouter markup factor and the same Claude subscription-vs-API break-even calculator.
- **Contact address — `hello@evaledger.com`** replaces the old support mailbox in the footer, the terms and the privacy policy.

### Unchanged

- **URLs.** Route slugs (`/token-cost-calculator`, `/openrouter-pricing-calculator`, `/claude-token-cost-calculator`, `/leaderboard`, `/releases`, `/series`) are untouched — every link you have shared keeps working.
- **The price feed.** `/api/prices.json` is unchanged and still the machine-readable source of truth.
- **Your account.** Sign-ins, subscriptions and saved sponsorship history carry over as they are; only the domain they live on changed.
